# HealthTrack System Manual

## Rural Health Unit of Pila, Laguna

### 1. Overview

HealthTrack is a role-based health service management system for RHU Pila. It helps patients access services, join the queue, track requests, view records, and manage follow-ups. Staff use the web console to manage queues, encode visits, perform consultations, maintain records, monitor inventory, and view reports and heat maps.

### 2. User Roles

- **Patient:** Registers, updates profile, selects a service, joins the queue, checks service status, views records, and monitors follow-ups.
- **BHW / Volunteer:** Encodes patients waiting for service and issues queue numbers.
- **Nurse:** Manages the queue and service desk, handles nurse-led services, records patient information, and monitors inventory and workflows.
- **Doctor:** Reviews the Doctor Consult queue, records diagnosis and prescription, completes consultations, and creates applicable follow-up schedules.
- **Account Manager:** Manages authorized user accounts.

### 3. Patient Portal Guide

1. Open the Patient Portal and register or sign in.
2. Complete the Profile page with accurate name, contact, birthdate, sex, and barangay information.
3. Choose a service from Service Info.
4. Complete the required service intake details.
5. Select **Get in Line** to join the BHW/Volunteer encoding line.
6. After staff encoding, open **My Queue** to view the issued ticket and live status.
7. Check **Service Status** for request progress and released documents.
8. Check **Medical Records** and **Follow-ups** for completed consultations and scheduled care.

### 4. BHW / Volunteer Encode Desk

1. Sign in to the Staff Console.
2. Open **Encode Desk**.
3. Select a patient in the Awaiting Encoding list.
4. Verify and complete the intake information.
5. Save the encoded information.
6. Select **Get Queue Number** to issue the patient a ticket for Doctor Consult or the appropriate service desk.

### 5. Nurse Workflow

1. Open **Queue** to view active tickets and call patients in the appropriate order.
2. Use **Service Desk** for nurse-led services such as health cards, permits, and related requests.
3. Open **Patient Records** to encode or update clinical information where authorized.
4. Use **Inventory** to monitor stock levels.
5. Use **Workflow** and **Reports** to monitor service activity and operational records.

### 6. Doctor Consultation

1. Open **Doctor Consult**.
2. Select a waiting, next, or called patient assigned to a doctor service.
3. Review the patient profile and intake details.
4. Call the patient when ready.
5. Record diagnosis, notes, prescription, and required clinical details.
6. Complete the consultation. HealthTrack marks the queue ticket completed and saves the patient record.
7. Create animal-bite, TB, or outpatient follow-up schedules when applicable.

### 7. Queue Statuses

- **Waiting:** Ticket is active and waiting for staff or doctor action.
- **Next:** Ticket is prepared to be called.
- **Called:** Patient has been called.
- **Skipped:** Patient was temporarily skipped.
- **Completed / Done:** Service or consultation is finished.
- **Cancelled:** Ticket was cancelled.

### 8. Heat Map and Reports

The Heat Map displays clinical and reported-case patterns by barangay. It helps RHU staff identify possible disease hotspots, observe trends, and support community-health planning.

1. Open **Heat Map** from the Staff Console.
2. Review map points and barangay-level case patterns.
3. Apply available clinical or disease filters.
4. Use the information with Reports to guide validation, outreach, monitoring, and response planning.

Heat map results are decision-support information. They do not replace clinical verification or official outbreak investigation procedures.

### 6. Quick Troubleshooting

**Queue ticket is not visible:** Refresh the Queue page, verify the patient was encoded, and check internet connectivity.

**Changes are pending sync:** Keep the browser open, reconnect to the internet, and wait for synchronization to finish.

**Patient cannot access a record:** Confirm the patient profile is linked to the correct account and that the consultation was completed.

**Heat map has no results:** Check the date/filter selection and confirm clinical or reported-case data has been recorded with barangay information.
